Sources

Heat
CrisisReady HeatReady API: ERA5-Land reanalysis (Copernicus C3S) from June 2023, Open-Meteo for the latest days and the 6-day forecast. Daily max/min temperature, WBGT, UTCI and heat index per woreda; anomalies against the API's 2016–2025 climatology.
Flooding
River discharge from GloFAS v4 (Copernicus Emergency Management Service) via the Open-Meteo Flood API, 1984 to a 30-day ensemble forecast, at the 14 gauges and OND trigger levels of OCHA's Ethiopia flood anticipatory-action project (Centre for Humanitarian Data, ds-aa-eth-flooding). RP2/RP5 levels are fitted here to 1984–2025 annual peaks. River network: HydroRIVERS v1.0 (HydroSHEDS; Lehner & Grill 2013, CC-BY 4.0).
Food prices
World Bank Real-Time Food Prices (RTFP), market level, release 2026-10-05 (microdata catalog 4483): 129 markets, monthly; months without a survey are World Bank estimates.
Population & age
LandScan Global 2024 (population); WorldPop R2025A age structure (CC-BY 4.0).
Climate context
El Niño months from the NOAA CPC Oceanic Niño Index (ONI ≥ +0.5 °C for 5 overlapping seasons).
Boundaries & basemap
OCHA COD-AB admin 3 (woredas); Mapbox, © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Associations shown between prices, heat and river flow are exploratory correlations, not causal estimates. See the README for methods and caveats.
